Purification

What happens between the source and the cap.

Four stages happen to every bottle before it leaves the plant — in this exact order.

01 · Source

Ground water draw

Tested ground water from a tested aquifer — sampled before every batch.

02 · RO

Reverse osmosis

Membrane filtration removes dissolved solids and trace contaminants down to the molecular level.

03 · UV

Ultraviolet sterilisation

UV exposure neutralises any remaining biological activity without adding chemicals.

04 · O₃

Ozonation

A final ozone pass keeps the water stable in the bottle until the moment you open it.
