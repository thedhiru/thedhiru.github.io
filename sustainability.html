The process

The actual purification sequence.

This is the order the water physically moves through — not a marketing loop.

01

Ground water draw

Water is drawn from a tested ground water source, sampled at the point of intake before each batch.

02

Reverse osmosis

Membrane filtration removes dissolved solids and trace contaminants down to the molecular level.

03

UV sterilisation

Ultraviolet exposure neutralises any remaining biological activity — without adding any chemical treatment.

04

Ozonation & sealing

A final ozone pass keeps the water stable, then bottles are sealed on the same line under hygienic conditions.
