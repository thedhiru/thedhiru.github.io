Our story

Premium water, honestly bottled.

Dropp is built on a single idea: a packaged drinking water brand that earns trust by showing its work — the source, the process, the standards, the certifications, all of it printed on the label and reproducible in the lab.

The brand

From the finest natural ground water, finished to a standard.

Dropp Water Private Limited was set up to do one thing well: bottle premium packaged drinking water. FSSAI licence and BIS certification (IS 14543) are yet to be published — our internal quality programme follows the same specifications in the meantime. The water is drawn from tested ground water, purified in four stages, and packaged in a facility that follows the same standards from intake to pallet.

Every batch is tested. Every claim on the label is one the team can stand behind. That is the only pitch we make.

The process

Four stages, in this order, every batch.

01 · Source

Ground water draw

Water is drawn from a tested ground water source, sampled at the point of intake before each batch.

02 · RO

Reverse osmosis

Membrane filtration removes dissolved solids and trace contaminants down to the molecular level.

03 · UV

Ultraviolet sterilisation

UV exposure neutralises biological activity in the water without adding any chemical treatment.

04 · O₃

Ozonation & bottling

A final ozone pass and immediate sealing under hygienic conditions — bottled at the plant.

What we hold to

Three rules we don't bend on.

100% Pure & Safe

Multi-stage purification through RO + UV + Ozonation. Nothing is added to the water — purity is the entire process.

Premium quality assured

Tested for purity at every batch. FSSAI and BIS certifications are yet to publish — our internal testing already follows the BIS specification for packaged drinking water. Test reports available on request for any batch.

Naturally refreshing

Drawn from the finest natural ground water and finished to a clean, balanced taste that needs nothing added to it.
