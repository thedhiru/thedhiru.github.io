Common questions

Before you write in.

How is Dropp purified?+
Every batch goes through four stages in order: ground water draw, reverse osmosis, ultraviolet sterilisation, and ozonation. The result is bottled on the same line under hygienic conditions.
Is Dropp FSSAI certified? What about BIS?+
FSSAI licence and BIS certification (IS 14543) are yet to be published for Dropp. Our internal quality programme already follows the BIS specification for packaged drinking water. Test reports for any batch in circulation are available on request through the contact form.
Do you offer wholesale pricing for cafés and offices?+
Yes — case and bulk pricing scale with volume. Reach out via the wholesale email above with your expected monthly volume and your city, and our team will send a rate card within two business days.
Which sizes are available?+
Dropp ships in five sizes: 200 ml, 500 ml, 1 L, 2 L, and a returnable 20 L office jar. All sizes use the same source and the same purification sequence. See the Products page for the full list.
Can I recycle the bottle through normal municipal collection?+
Yes — the bottle, cap, and label are all designed to go through standard PET recycling streams without special handling. The 20 L jar is returnable and is collected on the next delivery.

Packaged drinking water sourced from the finest natural ground water, purified through RO + UV + Ozonation, and bottled to quality standards (BIS certification in process). Pure water. Pure trust.
